# Marketing Campaign Performance Tracker & Dashboard

## Project Overview

An interactive Microsoft Excel project designed to track marketing campaign performance, compare marketing channels, and evaluate campaign efficiency through KPIs and visual reporting.

The project uses simulated marketing data for a fictional home-living brand, **Kiran Home Living**, to demonstrate practical Excel skills and marketing operations analysis.

## Key Features

- Interactive dashboard with marketing channel and campaign filters.
- KPI cards for marketing spend, attributed revenue, ROAS, conversions, and cost per acquisition.
- Visual comparison of marketing spend and attributed revenue across channels.
- Channel-level conversion analysis and campaign ROAS ranking.
- Formula-driven calculations using Excel functions and structured data.
- Executive dashboard formatted for A4 landscape printing.

## Workbook Structure

| Worksheet | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Campaign Data | Contains campaign performance records, including impressions, clicks, spend, leads, conversions, and attributed revenue. |
| Analysis | Calculates KPIs and prepares channel-level and campaign-level summaries. |
| Executive Dashboard | Presents interactive filters, KPI cards, charts, and campaign performance insights. |

## Key Marketing KPIs

- **CTR (Click-Through Rate):** Clicks ÷ Impressions
- **CPL (Cost per Lead):** Marketing Spend ÷ Leads
- **CPA (Cost per Acquisition):** Marketing Spend ÷ Conversions
- **ROAS (Return on Ad Spend):** Attributed Revenue ÷ Marketing Spend

The workbook uses error handling for calculations with zero denominators.

## Tools & Skills Demonstrated

- Microsoft Excel
- Excel Tables and structured references
- SUMIFS and IFERROR
- KPI calculations and performance tracking
- Interactive dropdown filters
- Excel charts and dashboard design
- Marketing data analysis and reporting

## Business Applications

The workbook can help users compare channel performance, identify campaigns with stronger or weaker ROAS, monitor marketing expenditure, and understand differences in conversion outcomes.

These insights can support marketing reporting and campaign review discussions. They do not establish that marketing spend caused the observed revenue.

## Data & Limitations

All data in this project is synthetic and intended for educational and portfolio purposes. It does not represent actual company results or verified industry benchmarks.

Attributed revenue is treated as revenue credited to a campaign within the simulated dataset. Actual attribution accuracy, profitability, and campaign effectiveness would require additional business context.

## How to Use

1. Download the Excel workbook.
2. Open it in desktop Microsoft Excel.
3. Navigate to the **Executive Dashboard** sheet.
4. Select a marketing channel and campaign using the dropdown filters.
5. Review the updated KPI cards and charts.
6. Explore the **Analysis** sheet to understand the underlying calculations.
